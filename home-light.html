Appointment privacy

When you request an appointment, your name, email, phone number, preferred treatment, date, time and any note are stored in the website’s booking database for handling your request.

Only share information needed to arrange your visit. Please do not include medical information. No payment information is collected, and submitting this form does not subscribe you to marketing.
